Manuscript: "Under aridification the binding constraint is inflow, not storage..." (Zambrano). Reviewed; verdict **Major Revision (leaning Reject in present form)**.

**Why:** The method (within-basin upstream/downstream placebo = H7) is the real, novel contribution. But the paper markets a substantive discovery the evidence can't carry.

**How to apply (the 5 blocking objections — re-check these each revision):**
1. Title claim "binding constraint is inflow, not storage" is a bare pooled time-series trend (table_storage_band.csv, reservoir FE only, no counterfactual, pooled ~22 reservoirs NOT the matched-21). Flat amplitude is equally consistent with rising demand drawdown. Demote to hypothesis OR require a naturalized-inflow series.
2. Whole-basin ET DiD is listed as a "null" but its cluster CI EXCLUDES zero (+0.048 [0.026,0.070]), p_perm=0.11, and it carries the pro-vulnerability sign. It is also the one outcome NOT given equivalence treatment in table_equivalence.csv. Selective retirement of an inconvenient result.
3. Equivalence: ALL rows of table_equivalence.csv = equivalent FALSE. Streamflow MDE 46-59% of baseline => a 20-45% (policy-relevant) buffer is NOT excluded. "Rule out buffering that would justify storage expansion" overclaims.
4. Aridity-curvature sensitivity (matched-controls.md): forcing-conditioned ATT swings 0.33 -> 0.11 under a QUADRATIC aridity term; residual aridity SMD 0.17 (>0.1). Linear/DR adjustment under-controls the confound. Must be in main text — the null may be an aridity artifact.
5. Upstream placebo internal validity: upstream gauges may differ in drainage area/elevation/snow fraction => up=down could be regime difference, not absence of regulation. Decisive regional collapse is driven by the SOUTH (7 treated/39 ctrl, p_perm 0.78) where effect is a priori smallest; arid north only 5 controls, "unstable."

Novelty verdict: identification advance (placebo) is novel; "inflow not storage" conclusion is NOT (restates Wang 2024 / Li 2023, both cited). Don't let methodological generality bleed into substantive generality.

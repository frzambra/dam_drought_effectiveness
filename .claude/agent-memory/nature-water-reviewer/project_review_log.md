2026-06-28 — First full NW review (written to docs/progress_summary/2026-06-28_nature-water-review.md).
Verdict: Reject as a positive-finding socio-hydrology paper; the convergent null + siting-confound + upstream placebo is publishable-grade methodology but NOT yet a Nature Water paper without (a) a formal equivalence/power test converting "we found no effect" into "we can rule out effects larger than X", and (b) a naturalized-inflow mass-balance counterfactual or operating-rule analysis to make the streamflow null mechanistic rather than design-limited.
Recommended path: reframe as a causal-cautionary + rigorous-null contribution ("reservoir siting, not regulation, drives apparent drought buffering in Chile"); the upstream placebo is the single most novel/defensible asset. Needs equivalence testing, the 2020-24 streamflow extension, and ideally the naturalized-inflow counterfactual to clear the NW bar. Otherwise strong fit for WRR / HESS / Nature Communications.

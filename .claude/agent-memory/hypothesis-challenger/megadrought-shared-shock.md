The study period (2005-2026) is dominated by the Chilean mega-drought (~2010-present). Almost all "severe ecological drought" tail events across the 26 dammed basins come from this ONE protracted shock.

**Why:** Tail/extreme-value and regime-switching estimates that look like "26 basins" of evidence are effectively a handful of (or one) correlated climate episode observed in parallel. Effective sample size for tail estimation is far below the nominal n. Spatially correlated forcing means basins are not independent draws.

**How to apply:** Whenever a claim rests on heavier tails, threshold crossings, or regime switches in dammed vs control basins, ask whether the "tail" is just the mega-drought shared across units. Demand block-bootstrap by drought EPISODE (not by basin-month), and check whether the effect survives if the 2010+ period is the only source of tail mass. Treat per-basin GPD fits during this window as near-unidentified. Related: [[tail-power-reality]].

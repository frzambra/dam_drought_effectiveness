The original hypothesis program (H1 tail rectifier, H2 induced demand, H3 spatial
displacement, H4 ecological lock-in) collapsed into a consistent NULL across four
independent estimators. The dominant empirical fact: **selection/siting dominates
everything** — reservoirs sit in arid, already-irrigated, hydrogeologically distinct
basins. The flatter SPEI->SSI buffering signature is present equally UPSTREAM
(physically unregulated) and downstream of dams, and dies under randomization inference.
Cropland concentration gap is stable across 2000-2024 (a siting signature, not an effect).

**Why:** Every old hypothesis treated siting endogeneity as a confound to purge en route
to a treatment effect. There is no surviving operational treatment effect to find.

**How to apply:** Do NOT propose new operational/buffering treatment-effect hypotheses —
they are observationally dead. Build on the siting-dominates result. The reframe that
clears the Nature Water bar:

- **H5 (recommended central narrative, score 9.0):** "Markers, not makers." Vulnerability
  is set at the siting/selection stage; conditional on pre-dam covariates the operational
  residual brackets zero. A credibly-estimated null *is* the finding.
- **H7 (score 8.3, the identification engine):** upstream/downstream within-basin placebo
  as a generalizable diagnostic that differences out siting. GATING STEP: audit how many of
  the 24 treated basins have BOTH upstream and downstream CR2 gauges — determines national
  result vs. subset demonstration.
- **H6 (score 8.3, transformative but risky):** damming changes WHAT drought means
  (conditional dependence structure: SPEI->outcome coupling decouples, storage->outcome
  appears) rather than its magnitude — explains why mean estimators (DiD/ATT) return null.
  RISK: unique "rebinds to allocation" content needs reservoir release/operating data
  (MISSING; only deltaS drawdown proxy on disk). Collapses into H5 if the upstream placebo
  erases the decoupling. Keep as upside option, never the foundation.

Recommended spine: H5 (headline) + H7 (engine) + H6 (mechanistic upside). Message:
"vulnerability is built at the drawing board, not the spillway."

Use permutation/randomization inference within Köppen x aridity strata throughout
(see user auto-memory [[h2-convergent-null]]; fwildclusterboot is off CRAN).
Identification is via continuous within-panel storage variation, NOT
staggered-DiD-on-commissioning (~21/26 reservoirs predate the 2005 panel).

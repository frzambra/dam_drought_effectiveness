The conventional policy claim "dams reduce drought impacts therefore build more dams"
is REJECTED. Two failures:
1. Premise fails our cleanest test (buffering is siting not regulation; upstream==downstream;
   see [[program-pivot-markers-not-makers]]).
2. Under aridification (Zambrano et al. 2025, "From Drought to Aridification", Earth's Future,
   doi:10.1029/2025EF006744 -- Chile crossed from episodic drought into PERSISTENT structural
   aridification, declining soil moisture + ET), the buffering premise assumes stationary
   recoverable drought.

**H8 — The Evaporating Buffer: RETIRED (tested 2026-06, null 4/4).**
H8 claimed refill EFFICIENCY (storage produced per unit surplus) DECAYS over time, fingerprint
= negative SPEI-surplus x year interaction. The coordinator ran it on cached targets
(storage_pct 2005-2024, spei3/spei12 per basin, 22 reservoirs/20 matched). Setup valid:
basin SPEI-12 declining -0.10/yr (real drying to detect against). Result NULL in every form:
- monthly dStorage~SPEI12 x year, reservoir+month FE: interaction -0.0006 p=0.57 (mid-sample
  transmission IS positive/real +0.013 p=0.027 -- refill works, slope just doesn't decay)
- SPEI-3: +0.0008 p=0.36; matched panel: -0.0009 p=0.51; annual gain x year: -0.034 p=0.33.
Refill efficiency is intact and within-year amplitude is flat (-0.004/yr p=0.61). Do NOT
spec-search to rescue it; the pre-registered falsifier (refill-per-surplus flat despite drying)
is exactly what was observed. Mechanism is dead.

**What IS real (but NOT a reservoir mechanism):** a whole-band DOWNSHIFT -- annual peak
-0.013/yr (p=0.014, survives controlling for surplus) AND trough drop together, amplitude
stable. Reservoirs are FED LESS (consistent with -0.10/yr SPEI) and/or drawn harder by demand.
Within-treated, no control, demand/release unobserved -> descriptive SUPPLY context, not a
causal reservoir effect.

**Reframed policy claim (the live, honest one):** reservoirs still convert surplus to storage
as well as ever -- the buffer is NOT degrading. The problem is the SUPPLY: less water arrives.
Binding constraint under aridification is INFLOW, not storage volume. Building more dams is a
supply-blind response -- adds tanks, not water. The argument is "evaporating SUPPLY," not
"evaporating buffer." Target demand/allocation, not new storage.

**Re-ranked spine (post-H8 retirement):**
- H5 markers-not-makers: 9.0 -- REINFORCED (refill-efficiency null removes the last
  reservoir-specific mechanism; even apparent decline is climate supply, not the dam).
- H7 upstream/downstream placebo: 8.3 -- identification engine, unchanged.
- H6 decoupling/regime-shift: DEMOTED to 7.8, below bar -> discussion only (storage-recovery
  shown stationary; its unique "rebinds to allocation" limb needs missing release data).
- H8: RETIRED.

**Central narrative:** H5 (markers not makers) + H7 (placebo engine), with aridification as a
SUPPLY ceiling. Message: "reservoirs still store water as well as ever -- there is just less
and less to store; under aridification the binding constraint is inflow not storage, so building
more dams is a supply-blind response, not resilience."
